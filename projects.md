# Projects

This directory contains step-by-step methodological walkthroughs, open-source experiment templates, and published reporting standards for learning Psychology by building tangible research artifacts.

> _What I cannot create, I do not understand — Richard Feynman._

Select a deliverable below to build hands-on methodological intuition and apply psychological theory to empirical practice.
